🎣 Phishing Awareness Training

Understand modern phishing techniques and prevention methods.

📌 What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack technique where attackers trick users into revealing sensitive information like passwords, banking details, OTPs, and personal data.

⚠️ Types of Phishing

📧 Email Phishing

Fake emails pretending to be banks or trusted companies.

📱 Smishing

SMS-based phishing attacks using fake text messages.

📞 Vishing

Voice phishing attacks through fraudulent phone calls.

🎯 Spear Phishing

Targeted phishing attacks aimed at specific individuals.

🏢 Business Email Compromise

Attackers impersonate company executives.

🌐 Clone Phishing

Fake copies of legitimate websites or emails.

📧 Fake Email Example

From: support@paypal-security-alert.com

Subject: Urgent Account Verification Required

Dear Customer,

Suspicious activity has been detected in your account. Verify immediately to avoid suspension.

Verify Account
  • ⚠️ Fake domain name
  • ⚠️ Urgent threatening message
  • ⚠️ Suspicious verification link
  • ⚠️ Emotional pressure tactics

🛡️ Prevention Tips

  • Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
  • Never share OTPs or passwords
  • Verify suspicious links before clicking
  • Use strong passwords
  • Keep systems updated
  • Use antivirus protection
